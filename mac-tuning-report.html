Observed result and scrutiny

Both-off versus baseline: four-worker median -7.0%; one-worker median +7.9%. These are descriptive differences, not established causal effects. A benefit at one concurrency level does not require a benefit at every level.

Within condition B alone, the first half of four-worker batches averaged 56.20s and the second half 51.00s (-9.3%), without a setting change. The fixed condition order cannot separate treatment effects from changing machine state.

Both no_log conditions had zero new or changed persistent-history files, while fseventsd continued consuming more than one CPU core on average. This verifies history suppression; it does not show that live event handling stopped. A quiet-machine, repeated crossover is needed to estimate performance effects.

Interpretation limits

  • One sequential block per condition, not randomized crossover; repeated batches are not independent repetitions of a setting change.
  • Surfshark antivirus was present but recorded near-zero cumulative CPU during scored intervals; the busy diagnostic snapshot came from an earlier attempt. Blocking or kernel overhead was not measured.
  • Spotify used roughly one CPU core during much of the experiment. Active swap-out page volume increased substantially between conditions.
  • Warm cache; scripts disabled; offline timed installs; no fsync.
  • no_log requests persistent-history suppression; live event processing continues.
  • Daemon counters cover the whole machine, with no idle baseline or per-volume attribution; high CPU alone does not establish a workload bottleneck.
  • CPU deltas can undercount restarted processes. Raw snapshots include preparation; reported scored intervals exclude setup, warmups and the first batch.
  • Spotlight enabled/disabled status was checked, but indexing of representative benchmark files was not verified.
  • No build or HMR compatibility validation in this experiment.
  • One short metadata-only history verification ran during condition D; all samples are retained.

The three stage medians are independent and need not sum to the median total.
